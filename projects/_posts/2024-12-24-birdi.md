### Summary

The  BIRDI study aims to combine information from brain scans and routine health data in patients who have previously had a traumatic brain injury (TBI), to understand what factors predict outcome and recovery. This will be used to develop a tool to estimate survival for patients with different types of brain injury and may be helpful in supporting discussions between doctors and families in the future. To find out more click [here](https://birdi.sustainsw.ac.uk/the_project.html).
